A personal logbook for your work.

Keep project notes, record your daily work, and track your time. Your notes are plain-text documents you can open and edit with other apps.

Working in WorkLog

Notes and daily logs
Organize notes by task and keep dated daily logs. Open notes in tabs and view images, PDFs, and Mermaid diagrams alongside your writing.
Checklists and search
See unfinished checklist items from your notes in one overview. Search across active and closed tasks, then jump to the matching lines.
Time tracking and reports
Enter time manually or copy elapsed time from the timer. Review totals by day and project code to help fill in your timesheet. Edit past entries and export reports as Markdown.

Storage & backups

Your notes, usable
beyond WorkLog.

Your notes use open formats: Markdown and plain text. Open and edit them with any text editor, even if you stop using WorkLog. Time records are also readable as plain text. Images and PDFs keep their original formats.

Back up to a folder or Git remote, manually or daily while WorkLog is open. Git is optional: add it for note version history and remote backups.

Ask an assistant about your work

Connect a compatible AI assistant to summarize your work or draft a review from saved notes and time entries. Access uses MCP and is read-only. Sharing is off each time you open WorkLog; start it in Settings.
